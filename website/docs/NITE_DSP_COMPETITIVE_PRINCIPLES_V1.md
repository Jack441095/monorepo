# NITE DSP — COMPETITIVE PRINCIPLES V1 (Phase 1, continued)

Principles extracted from the reference set. Nothing copied — each is
translated into a NITE DSP rule.

| Company | Extracted principle | NITE DSP application |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Apple** | Polish = restraint + perfect defaults. Trust through calm consistency. | One radius scale, one spacing rhythm, one motion feel. Never ship a "clever" surface that breaks the system. |
| **Ableton** | Musician-first: the workflow is the marketing. Show the product doing real work. | Demos and workflow timelines lead; prose supports. Panels look like the app. |
| **FabFilter** | Precision instrument aesthetic: dense, flat, perfectly aligned controls; zero decoration. | Professional information density; 1px structures; controls that look machined. |
| **Native Instruments** | Hardware heritage made software: dark surfaces, backlit accents, product-as-instrument. | Keep the studio-hardware frame language; backlit accents only for live signal. |
| **Linear** | Keyboard-first professional software; opinionated typography; speed as a feature. | Visible keyboard affordances; instant demo states; no fake loading; tight type scale. |
| **Raycast** | Command-surface thinking: everything one keystroke away; empty states are designed. | Demos expose their controls up front; designed idle states (never blank). |
| **Vercel** | Developer credibility: ship-log honesty, roadmap transparency, monochrome confidence. | Roadmap section; honest status chips; engineering receipts in copy. |
| **Teenage Engineering** | Emotional hardware identity; playful precision; the product IS the brand object. | The NITE Signal language stays — it is the emotional layer. Restrained, never neon. |

## Cross-cutting rules adopted

1. **Show the instrument, not the brochure.** Every page leads with a
   product surface (demo, panel, timeline).
2. **Speed is a trust signal.** No artificial loading anywhere; idle states
   are designed and instantly interactive.
3. **Density with hierarchy.** Professional tools may be dense — hierarchy
   (type roles, 1px structure, spacing rhythm) keeps density calm.
4. **Honesty as aesthetic.** Status chips, confidence language, receipts —
   truthfulness is part of the visual identity.
5. **One ecosystem story.** Products are instruments; the intelligence layer
   is the power supply; the website explains the system, not just items.
