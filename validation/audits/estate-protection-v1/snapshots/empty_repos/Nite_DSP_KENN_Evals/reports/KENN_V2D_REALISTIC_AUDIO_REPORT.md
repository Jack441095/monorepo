# Realistic Synthetic Generalisation

232 deterministic, mix-like cases covered bright/dark/bass-heavy/dynamic/
dense/wide/narrow/ambient/drum-heavy/asymmetric styles alongside clipping,
headroom, persistent/alternating L/R, and multi-fault fixtures. Product-decoder
candidate parity remained 100%; healthy FP remained 0%.

The aggregate recall was 57.1% because the corpus includes DC, bass, resonance
and phase families deliberately outside V2-D's three integrated candidates.
This is an honest scope boundary, not a detector regression claim. Style alone
was not considered a defect.
