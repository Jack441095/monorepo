# Headroom

V2-A recall was 0%. The product threshold checks a sample-peak condition, but
the V2-A headroom fixture did not reliably cross that product threshold. This
is a ground-truth/threshold mismatch, not a basis for changing copy.

The V2-B experimental gate makes headroom a separate detector with observe,
recommend, and strong boundaries. On the frozen V2 holdout it detected 9/11
labelled headroom cases (81.8%) with no healthy recommendations. This is
evidence for a future production measurement repair, not a production result.
