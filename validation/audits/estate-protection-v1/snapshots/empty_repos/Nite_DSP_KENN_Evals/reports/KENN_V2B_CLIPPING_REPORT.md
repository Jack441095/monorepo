# Clipping

V2-A clipping recall was 5/15 (33.3%). The experimental gate distinguishes a
hot signal from a measured clipping candidate and applies lower intervention
thresholds only to the clipping detector. Holdout result: 13/15 (86.7%)
candidate coverage, with no healthy recommendations. It does not claim to
separate intentional saturation from clipping without an explicit confounder.
