# SLO AI-Infra Proposals — 2026-09-18 (PROPOSALS ONLY — nothing implemented)
All local-first. Cloud-audio/cloud-metadata ideas: **none proposed**; any that surface later are P0-privacy-reject per R7. Privacy audit result: existing CorrectionLog.h explicitly stores path+metadata only, never audio — verified.

## Hard evidence constraint (read first)
`Source/CorrectionLog.h` header: by-ear labels are the **only** intervention that ever improved SLO on unseen libraries (+3.29pp accuracy, −17.9pp false-accept per 500). Nine encoders, six feature families, LoRA, GroupDRO, DANN, CORAL, augmentation, factorised taxonomy, taxonomy expansion **all produced nothing**. Any AI-infra proposal must argue why it escapes that graveyard or be explicitly filed as research-with-kill-criteria.
