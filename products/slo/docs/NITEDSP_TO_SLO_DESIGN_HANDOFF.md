# NITE DSP Website V3 to SLO Handoff

The website establishes a dark surface hierarchy, compact technical labels,
large direct headlines, a restrained green operational accent, and a blue
product/discovery accent. SLO V3 should use these as design input, not as a
mandate to duplicate web layouts.

- Preserve the stepped background/panel/border hierarchy.
- Use the green accent for active selection and playback, with amber reserved
  for experimental or cautionary actions.
- Use blue for product identity and discovery context, not for destructive or
  real-time operational states.
- Prefer dense, readable information with a clear primary action over card
  grids or decorative effects.
- Treat map points as an audio-discovery language: category is secondary;
  selected, playing and related states need independent treatment.
- Keep copy short, concrete and production-aware.
