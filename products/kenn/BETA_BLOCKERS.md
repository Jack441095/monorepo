## Resolved

| ID | Blocker | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| BB-4 | The local Mix Review adapter called `audio_analysis.mix_review.analyze_wav`'s `review_flags()` — an unevaluated heuristic engine with ~12+ flags (low dynamics, low-mid buildup, perceived harshness, mono risk, wide sides, etc.) — and passed all of them through unfiltered. **None of those flags have precision/recall evidence; only clipping, headroom, and lr_imbalance do, and that evidence belongs to a separate, unintegrated detector module** (`products/kenn-evaluation/benchmark/measured_candidates_v2c.py` + `recommendation_gate.py`, gated behind `KENN_MEASURED_GATE_V2C`, never wired into product code per V2-D). This meant the adapter, as built, would have shown testers unqualified findings under the guise of evidence-backed ones — a direct violation of "identify only qualified audio issues." Fixed by promoting the actual qualified detector+gate logic into `products/kenn/mix-review/qualified_detectors.py` (frozen copy, provenance documented in-file) and rewiring `adapter.py` to decode via the real product decoder and run only those 3 detectors, contract schema bumped to `local_receipt.v2`. 12 new tests cover healthy/clipping/headroom/lr_imbalance/silence/corrupted/unsupported-format/oversized cases; 54/54 tests pass across `chat/`, `mix-review/`, `automix/`. |
