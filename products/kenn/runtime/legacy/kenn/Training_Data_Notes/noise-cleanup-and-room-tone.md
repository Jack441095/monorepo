# Noise Cleanup And Room Tone

Type: Editing workflow
Tags: noise, noisy, cleanup, room tone, podcast, dialogue, vocals, editing, restoration, artifacts
Status: Approved

Short answer:
Clean noisy dialogue by reducing the problem without creating artifacts or making the audio sound processed. Use editing, fades, gates, expansion, and gentle noise reduction before heavy restoration.

Try this:
1. Remove obvious clicks, bumps, breaths, and dead-space noise manually where possible.
2. Add short fades on edits so cuts do not click.
3. Use room tone under edited speech if silence feels unnatural.
4. Use a gate or expander lightly; avoid chopping off word endings or breaths too aggressively.
5. Apply noise reduction in small amounts and compare before/after at normal listening volume.
6. If the recording is very noisy, improve the source recording before relying on plugins.

Why it matters:
Over-cleaned audio can sound worse than slightly noisy audio. Natural editing keeps speech and vocals believable while still reducing distractions.
