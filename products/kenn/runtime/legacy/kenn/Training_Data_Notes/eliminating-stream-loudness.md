# Eliminating Stream Loudness

Type: Signal chain
Tags: mastering, compression, delay, reverb
Status: Approved
Reviewed: 2026-07-01

Short answer:
Eliminate stream loudness by using a compressor and adding subtle delay and reverb to create a dramatic intro sound.

Try this:
1. Apply a compressor to the audio signal.
2. Add a slight delay with a short duration (e.g., 50 ms) to introduce some space.
3. Use a small amount of reverb to enhance the stereo image without overpowering the main elements.
4. Adjust the compression settings for optimal balance between volume control and overall sound quality.

Why it matters:
Eliminating stream loudness ensures that all listeners have equal access to the audio, making your content more inclusive and enjoyable for everyone.

Common mistakes:
- Overusing delay or reverb can make the intro sound too busy.
- Not adjusting the compressor settings correctly can result in an uneven volume distribution throughout the track.
