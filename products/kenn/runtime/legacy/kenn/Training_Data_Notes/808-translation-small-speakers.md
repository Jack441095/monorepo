# 808 Translation On Small Speakers

Type: Low-end translation workflow
Tags: 808, bass, sub, small speakers, phone, harmonics, saturation, mono, kick, translation, mixing
Status: Approved

Short answer:
An 808 translates on small speakers when the sub is clean but the upper harmonics are audible. Keep the fundamental controlled, add harmonics above the sub range, and check the relationship between kick, 808, and vocal in mono.

Try this:
1. Tune the 808 to the song key and remove notes that fight the chord movement.
2. Keep the deepest sub mostly mono and avoid widening the low end.
3. Add gentle saturation or parallel distortion so the 808 has audible harmonics on phones and laptops.
4. High-pass or EQ non-bass instruments so the 808 is not masked by low-mid clutter.
5. Sidechain or manually duck the 808 only where the kick needs impact.
6. Check on small speakers; you should hear the pitch or rhythm of the 808 even if the sub weight is missing.

Why it matters:
Small speakers cannot reproduce deep sub reliably. Harmonics, arrangement space, and mono-compatible low end make the bass line readable across playback systems.
