# Podcast Dialogue Edit In Ableton

Type: Podcast workflow
Tags: podcast, dialogue, editing, vocal, noise, leveling, cleanup
Status: Approved

Short answer:
Edit podcast audio in order: remove obvious junk cuts, level speech for consistency, then light cleanup (EQ and gentle compression). Music and ads sit underneath a stable dialogue bus.

Try this:
1. Place dialogue on one or more tracks; group to a **Dialogue** bus for shared processing.
2. Cut breaths, stumbles, and long silences at zero-crossings or short fades (3–10 ms) to avoid clicks.
3. Use clip gain or volume automation on hot lines before heavy bus compression.
4. On the Dialogue bus: EQ Eight high-pass around 80–100 Hz; cut narrow resonances if the room sounds boxy.
5. Add Compressor (ratio 2:1–3:1, medium attack, auto release) aiming for 2–4 dB gain reduction on speech.
6. Optional: light De-esser or multiband dip around 5–8 kHz if sibilance is harsh.
7. Keep music on a separate bus 6–12 dB under speech during VO; automate music down under intros if needed.
8. Bounce the episode in WAV; keep the Live Set for insert revisions.
