# Vocal Compression Chain

Type: Production workflow
Tags: vocal, vocals, compression, compressor, mixing, dynamics, eq
Status: Approved

Short answer:
Treat vocal compression in stages: level the performance first, then use gentle serial compression (or one well-tuned compressor) before EQ and time-based effects on sends.

Try this:
1. Clip gain or gain automation so phrases sit in a consistent range before heavy compression.
2. First compressor: slower attack (10–30 ms), medium release, 2–4 dB gain reduction on peaks for control.
3. Second stage (optional): faster attack for peak control, or a single compressor with 3–6 dB GR if the vocal is already even.
4. High-pass around 80–100 Hz on vocals that do not need sub; de-ess after compression if sibilance increases.
5. Send reverb and delay from a bus; keep the dry vocal present for clarity.

Why it matters:
Compressing vocals before they are levelled forces the compressor to fight performance swings. A short chain with clear intent sounds more natural than one extreme plugin setting.
