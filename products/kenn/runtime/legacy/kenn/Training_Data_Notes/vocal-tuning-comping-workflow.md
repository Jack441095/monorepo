# Vocal Tuning And Comping Workflow

Type: Vocal production workflow
Tags: vocals, tuning, comping, pitch correction, timing, edit, recording, performance
Status: Approved

Short answer:
Comp the best performance before heavy tuning. Fix timing and obvious pitch problems first, then tune only as much as the style needs. A natural vocal usually comes from good take selection, not from forcing one weak take into shape.

Try this:
1. Organise takes and mark the strongest phrases before editing.
2. Build a comp from emotion, timing, tone, and lyric clarity, not just perfect pitch.
3. Clean breaths, clicks, and bad edits before detailed tuning.
4. Tune exposed notes more carefully than buried doubles or stacks.
5. Keep transitions between tuned notes smooth unless the genre wants a hard tuned sound.
6. Listen in the full mix; over-tuning that sounds fine solo can feel lifeless with the track.

Why it matters:
Tuning works best when it supports a strong performance. If comping is rushed, pitch correction has to solve emotional, timing, and tone problems that should have been fixed at the take-selection stage.
