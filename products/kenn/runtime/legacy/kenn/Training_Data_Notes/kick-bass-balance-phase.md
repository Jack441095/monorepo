# Kick Bass Balance And Phase

Type: Mixing workflow
Tags: kick, bass, sub, low-end, phase, sidechain, eq, balance, mono, mixing
Status: Approved

Short answer:
Make the kick and bass share the low end by choosing who owns the sub, checking phase, and controlling overlap with level, EQ, arrangement, or sidechain. Do not keep boosting both; decide which element leads the bottom end.

Try this:
1. Level-match kick and bass before adding plugins.
2. Decide whether the kick fundamental or bass/sub owns the lowest energy.
3. Check polarity or phase if the low end disappears when both play together.
4. Use EQ only where there is real overlap; avoid carving huge holes by default.
5. Use sidechain compression or volume shaping if the kick needs space on every hit.
6. Check mono and small speakers to confirm the groove still translates.

Why it matters:
Low-end problems are often arrangement and phase problems, not just EQ problems. A clear kick/bass relationship makes the whole mix feel louder and cleaner.
