# Making Rhythmically Accented Percussion

Type: Performance workflow
Tags: Subs, Rumble, Rhythmic accents, Side chaining, Volume envelope
Status: Approved
Reviewed: 2026-07-01

Short answer:
To add rhythmic accents to percussion loops, use side-chain triggering with a low-frequency signal and adjust volume envelopes for tightness.

Try this:
1. Place the rim shot on the two and four beats.
2. Use Serum or another plugin to create a side-chain trigger based on the sub signal.
3. Adjust the volume envelope of the rim shots slightly shorter than other elements in the drum loop.
4. Add some swing to these elements for added groove.

Why it matters:
Rhythmic accents can significantly enhance the overall rhythm and feel of a drum loop, making it more engaging and dynamic.

Common mistakes:
- Using too much reverb or delay that drowns out the rhythmic elements.
- Not adjusting volume envelopes properly to maintain tightness without overpowering other parts.
