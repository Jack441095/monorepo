# Adding Overtones

Type: Mixing technique
Tags: Sub bass, Harmonics, Volume control
Status: Approved
Reviewed: 2026-07-01

Short answer:
Adding more overtones can help balance the waveform and reduce distortion, while harmonics add warmth without increasing volume.

Try this:
1. Add a second auto overtone.
2. Adjust the level of the added overtones to match the first one.
3. Consider adding even harmonics for additional warmth.
4. Normalize the sub bass section to ensure it's as loud as possible.

Why it matters:
Adding overtones and harmonics can create a fuller, more balanced sound in your mix without overwhelming the volume, making your tracks stand out while maintaining clarity.

Common mistakes:
- Adding too many overtones or harmonics can clutter the mix.
- Not balancing the levels properly can lead to an uneven mix.
