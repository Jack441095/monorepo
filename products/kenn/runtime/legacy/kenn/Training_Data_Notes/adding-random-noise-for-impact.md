Short answer:
Adding random noise subtly enhances a track by introducing unexpected elements that catch the ear. This technique is particularly effective in small doses and can add depth without overpowering the overall mix.

Try this:
1. Start with a low level of random noise (around -6 dB) added to isolated sections, such as stems or individual instruments.
2. Gradually increase the volume until it becomes noticeable but not intrusive.
3. Experiment by adding different types of noise (like Noisia effects) for varying results.

Why it matters:
This technique can make a track stand out without being overly dramatic, making it perfect for subtle adjustments that enhance the listener's experience without altering the core song structure.

Common mistakes:
- Overdoing it and making the noise too prominent.
- Not considering the impact on the overall mix balance.
