# Creating Space with Reverb

Type: Mixing technique  
Tags: Stereo placement, Creative effect, Reverb application  
Status: Approved  
Reviewed: 2026-07-01  

Short answer:
By adding reverb to a bass line and layering it throughout the mix, you can create more space and depth, enhancing the stereo image without overpowering other elements.

Try this:
1. Add subtle reverb to the bass line in the initial stage.
2. Layer reverb on different parts of the track (e.g., base, snare).
3. Use a specific layer for creating stereo high end.
4. Mute layers temporarily to isolate effects and focus on one aspect at a time.

Why it matters:
This technique allows you to enhance the space in your mix without making the bass line sound too heavy or muddy. It's particularly effective when used creatively, as demonstrated by adding reverb to the bass line for a dramatic effect that adds depth and interest to the track.

Common mistakes:
- Overusing reverb can make elements sound muddled.
- Not layering effects correctly can result in an uneven mix.
