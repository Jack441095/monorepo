# Sub Bass Saturation

Type: Production workflow
Tags: bass, sub, saturation, saturator, low-end, harmonics, mixing
Status: Approved

Short answer:
Saturate sub bass to add upper harmonics so the note is audible on small speakers, but keep the fundamental clean and leave headroom on the mix bus.

Try this:
1. High-pass non-bass elements that do not need sub energy.
2. On the bass or sub group, use Saturator in Soft mode or Pedal with light drive — aim for colour, not obvious distortion.
3. Listen in mono: if the sub disappears, reduce drive or blend dry/wet.
4. High-cut the saturated signal above ~200–300 Hz if the top end gets harsh on the bass channel.
5. Sidechain the bass to the kick so the sub clears when the kick hits.

Why it matters:
Pure sub without harmonics vanishes on laptops and earbuds. Gentle saturation adds presence without replacing proper level and sidechain discipline.
