# Mix Translation Checks

Type: Mixing workflow
Tags: translation, reference, monitoring, mono, phone, car, earbuds, loudness, balance, mastering
Status: Approved

Short answer:
Translation means the mix keeps its balance across different playback systems. Check references, mono, quiet listening, small speakers, headphones, and a few real-world systems before assuming the mix is finished.

Try this:
1. Level-match one or two reference tracks in the same style.
2. Listen quietly; if the vocal, snare, or main hook disappears, rebalance before mastering.
3. Check mono for phase and width problems.
4. Check small speakers or phone playback for vocal, snare, and bass audibility.
5. Check headphones for harshness, sibilance, stereo tricks, and low-end exaggeration.
6. Leave headroom and avoid solving translation problems with a final limiter.

Why it matters:
A mix can sound impressive in one room and fail everywhere else. Translation checks reveal balance, phase, and tonal problems before delivery.
