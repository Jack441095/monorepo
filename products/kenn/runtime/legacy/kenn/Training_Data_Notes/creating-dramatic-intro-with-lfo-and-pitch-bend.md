# Creating Dramatic Intro with LFO and Pitch Bend

Type: Mixing technique
Tags: Sound design workflow, Creative technique
Status: Approved
Reviewed: 2026-07-01

Short answer:
To create a dramatic intro using LFO and pitch bend, start by setting up an ARP synth or similar slow-moving instrument. Use the LFO to gradually speed up or slow down the sound over time, creating a sense of movement and tension. Apply pitch bends to add depth and complexity to the melody or lead parts. Combine these elements with cinematic background tracks for added impact.

Try this:
1. Start with an ARP synth set at a low tempo.
2. Use LFO to slowly increase speed throughout the intro.
3. Add pitch bend effects to modulate the sound's pitch slightly.
4. Incorporate a dramatic spoken word or narrative over a steady, cinematic background track.

Why it matters:
This technique can instantly elevate a simple introduction into something truly memorable and engaging for your audience.

Common mistakes:
- Not using enough variation in speed changes; sounds become monotonous.
- Lack of consistent pitch modulation; the sound becomes too static.
