# EQ Eight Mixing Basics

Type: Production workflow
Tags: eq, eq eight, mixing, frequency, cleanup, balance
Status: Approved

Short answer:
Use EQ Eight for subtractive fixes first (remove rumble, harshness, or boxiness), then small boosts for tone. Change one band at a time and level-match so louder EQ does not fool your ear.

Try this:
1. High-pass non-bass elements that do not need sub energy (often 80–120 Hz on guitars, higher on hats).
2. Cut narrow problem frequencies with a bell band (scan with boosted Q, then cut where it sounds worst).
3. Use gentle wide boosts (+2 dB max) for presence (2–5 kHz) or air (10 kHz+) only when needed.
4. Compare bypass often; if bypass sounds better, the EQ move was wrong.
5. On the master bus, prefer tiny moves or skip EQ until the mix is balanced.

Why it matters:
EQ questions are frequent but manual retrieval returns device docs. This workflow note anchors answers in how you actually mix client projects.
