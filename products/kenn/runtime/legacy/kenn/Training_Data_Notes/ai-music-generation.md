# AI Music Generation

Type: Creative technique
Tags: AI music, generative AI, music creation, audio engineering, creative process
Status: Approved
Reviewed: 2026-07-01

Short answer:
AI-generated music can be a useful tool for producers to generate new ideas and experiment with different sounds without the constraints of traditional recording methods.

Try this:
1. Use AI to create unique vocal patterns or melodies that you can then modify and expand upon.
2. Experiment with prompts like "rhythm hyperlitch" to get creative with rhythm variations.
3. Upload loops with AI-generated suggestions for inspiration on how to continue your project.
4. Open old projects to explore new directions based on the audio quality of AI-generated ideas.

Why it matters:
AI can provide fresh perspectives and unexpected results, helping producers break free from traditional limitations and explore uncharted territories in music creation.

Common mistakes:
- Not utilizing AI creatively by focusing solely on improving existing tracks without exploring new concepts.
- Overusing AI to the point where it becomes a crutch for uninspired work.
