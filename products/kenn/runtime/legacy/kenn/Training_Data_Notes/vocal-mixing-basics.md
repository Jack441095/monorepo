# Vocal Mixing Basics

Type: Production workflow
Tags: vocals, eq, compression, de-essing, reverb, delay
Status: Approved

Short answer:
A basic vocal chain usually starts with cleanup, then compression, then tone shaping, then space effects like reverb and delay.

Try this:
1. Set the vocal level before adding plugins.
2. Use EQ Eight to remove rumble and obvious mud, but avoid making the vocal thin.
3. Use gentle compression to even out performance volume.
4. Add de-essing if S sounds are sharp.
5. Use reverb and delay on return tracks so the vocal stays clear.
6. Compare against a reference track at a similar loudness.

Why it matters:
Most vocal problems come from balance, recording quality, and over-processing. Fix the level and tone before adding big effects.
