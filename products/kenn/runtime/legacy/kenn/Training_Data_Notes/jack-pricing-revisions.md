# Jack Gandy Client Pricing and Revision Policy

Type: Business workflow
Tags: jack, pricing, rates, revision, project, scope, budget, hourly, mix price
Status: Approved
Source: Jack Gandy Personal Workflow
Reviewed: 2026-07-07

Short answer:
Jack Gandy charges a flat rate of **$150 per mix**. This rate includes up to **3 rounds of revisions** free of charge. Any additional revisions beyond the first 3 rounds are billed at an hourly rate of **$50/hour**.

Try this:
1. Include this pricing and revision structure in initial project quotes to set client expectations:
   - "Rate: $150 per mix. Includes up to 3 revisions. Additional revisions billed at $50/hour."
2. Document each revision round received from the client.
3. Consolidate client feedback to make each revision round as efficient and clear as possible.
4. Notify the client in advance if they are approaching the end of their 3 included rounds.

Why it matters:
A flat fee with capped revision rounds protects the engineer from "revision hell" (infinite minor tweaks that stall a project) while giving the client fair, predictable pricing. The hourly rate ensures payment for excessive time spent.

Common mistakes:
- Doing infinite free revisions without tracking rounds, which devalues the engineer's time and dilutes the per-mix rate.
- Charging for revisions without notifying the client beforehand that they have exceeded their limit.

When this does not apply:
- When negotiating bulk rates for long-term clients or full albums (which may have custom pricing terms).
