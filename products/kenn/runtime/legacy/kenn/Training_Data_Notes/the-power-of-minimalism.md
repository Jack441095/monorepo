Short answer:
By reducing the number of elements in your mix or track, you can focus on key instruments and vocals, creating a cleaner, more impactful sound that stands out.

Try this:
1. Remove unnecessary effects plugins from your session to streamline your setup.
2. Choose one instrument per layer for each part (e.g., bass, drums, lead) instead of trying to balance everything in one track.
3. Use fewer samples and loops, focusing on the most essential elements that contribute to your overall vision.

Why it matters:
Reducing choices can help you make more informed decisions about what really works in a mix or song, leading to better sound design and production skills over time.

Common mistakes:
- Adding too many effects plugins can clutter your session.
- Trying to balance everything in one track instead of focusing on key instruments and vocals.
