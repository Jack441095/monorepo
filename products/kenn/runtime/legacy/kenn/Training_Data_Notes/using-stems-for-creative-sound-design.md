# Using stems for creative sound design

Type: Sound Design Workflow
Tags: stem usage, drum manipulation, bass pitch envelope, low-mid notch filter, wide elements in bass
Status: Approved
Reviewed: 2026-07-01

Short answer:
Downloading and analyzing the stems of a favorite track can provide valuable insights into sound design techniques.

Try this:
1. Download and analyze the stems of your favorite Noisia song.
2. Identify specific elements like centricity, bass placement, and kick timing.
3. Apply these insights to your own tracks, experimenting with different variations.
4. Record your findings for future reference or share them on social media.

Why it matters:
By understanding how others manipulate sounds in their music, you can create more impactful and unique compositions of your own.

Common mistakes:
- Not taking the time to thoroughly analyze stems before applying changes.
- Overcomplicating sound design by adding too many effects without considering the overall balance.
