# Making a Dramatic Intro with Ableton

Type: Mixing technique
Tags: Granular processing, Reverb, Sub-bass enhancement, Atmosphere addition, Piano integration
Status: Approved
Reviewed: 2026-07-01

Short answer:
Start with a granular pad from the movies. Use Face Plan's granular sampler to adjust parameters like speed and pitch. Hold down a minor chord for about 2 minutes while adding reverb and low sub bass. Introduce atmosphere elements like rain sounds and wind chimes, followed by a long piano theme. Gradually add more pads and chords as the intro rises.

Why it matters:
This technique creates an immersive and dramatic sound that can captivate audiences, making your DJ or live show stand out.

Common mistakes:
- Not adjusting parameters correctly for granular processing.
- Forgetting to add reverb and low sub bass.
- Adding too many atmospheric elements without a clear musical progression.
- Not integrating the piano theme smoothly into the intro.
