Short answer:
By modulating the delay time of a delay effect and adjusting its pitch, you can create interesting textures and dynamics in your audio tracks.

Try this:

1. Use a delay pedal or software plugin to set up a basic delay with a fixed duration.
2. Slow down the delay time gradually while maintaining the same amount of delay as before.
3. Adjust the pitch of the delayed signal slightly upwards, creating a cool "wet laser" effect.
4. Experiment by combining different modulation rates and types (e.g., adding more pitch modulation or changing to a tremolo).

Why it matters:
This technique adds depth and complexity to your audio, making your tracks sound more dynamic and engaging.

Common mistakes:
- Not slowing down the delay time enough can result in too much of an effect.
- Over-modulating the pitch can make the track sound harsh or unnatural.
