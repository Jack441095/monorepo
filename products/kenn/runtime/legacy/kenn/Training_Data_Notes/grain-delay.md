# Grain Delay

Type: Mixing technique  
Tags: Grain Delay, Sound Design Workflow  
Status: Approved  
Reviewed: 2026-07-01  

Short answer:
Grain delay is a powerful mixing technique that adds depth and character to your audio tracks by creating small time delays for each grain in the sample. This effect can be used to create interesting textures and enhance the overall sound of your mix.

Try this:
1. Set up a new audio track as the input.
2. Record a short section of material, such as a drum loop or vocal phrase.
3. Use the Grain Delay effect on the recorded material.
4. Adjust parameters like decay time, grain size, and feedback to achieve the desired sound.

Why it matters:
Grain delay can add a unique character to your tracks by creating subtle variations in pitch and timing that mimic natural sounds. This technique is particularly effective for adding depth and complexity to drum loops or vocal performances.

Common mistakes:
- Overusing grain delay can result in a cluttered mix.
- Not adjusting the decay time correctly can lead to harsh, unnatural delays.
