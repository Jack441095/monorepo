Short answer:
The producer should use LFOs to control pitch and envelope parameters, applying effects like distortion and delay in different ways for varied results.

Try this:
1. Use an LFO on the coarse pitch at maximum range.
2. Adjust volume envelopes and apply filters as needed.
3. Layer a sine wave with an envelope for initial impact.
4. Add a comp filter to create flanging effects.
5. Panning and modulation of unison waves can enhance width.

Why it matters:
This technique allows for creating unique, granular sounds that are both versatile and dynamic, making the producer's workflow more creative and efficient.

Common mistakes:
- Not using LFOs effectively or layering too many effects simultaneously.
- Overcomplicating the sound design process without a clear goal in mind.
