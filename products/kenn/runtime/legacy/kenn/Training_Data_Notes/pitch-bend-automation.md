# Pitch Bend Automation

Type: Mixing technique  
Tags: Sound design workflow, Performance workflow, Signal chain  
Status: Approved  
Reviewed: 2026-07-01  

Short answer:
Start by holding down a note in the key of D# sharp for an extended duration. Then add pitchbend automation to gradually move up and back down quickly before landing on the final note. Finally, use a low-frequency oscillator (LFO) with a saw waveform that starts fast and slows down over time to create a plucky effect.

Why it matters:
This technique helps in creating dynamic and engaging intro sections by adding subtle pitch changes and volume modulation, enhancing the overall sound quality and listener engagement.

Common mistakes:
- Overcomplicating the automation process, leading to cluttered and difficult-to-manage signal chains.
- Not properly fine-tuning the ending point of the LFO to avoid abrupt transitions that can make the track feel unnatural.
