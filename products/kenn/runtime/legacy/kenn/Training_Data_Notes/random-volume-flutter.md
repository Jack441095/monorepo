Short answer:
To add a dynamic and unpredictable volume effect without overloading speakers or ears, simply apply a fast Low Frequency Oscillator (LFO) on an audio track's gain knob. This creates random volume fluctuations that can be split for stereo effects, with the cap set at 50% to avoid clipping.

Why it matters:
This technique adds excitement and character to tracks without requiring complex processing or additional hardware, making it a versatile tool for producers looking to enhance their mixes.

Common mistakes:
- Not using an LFO with enough speed can result in too much volume fluctuation.
- Setting the cap too low might cause clipping.
