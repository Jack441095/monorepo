# Creating Rumble with White Noise

Type: Mixing technique  
Tags: Drum processing, Reverb, EQ, Shaper Box, Subtractive eq  
Status: Approved  
Reviewed: 2026-07-01  

Short answer:
The key idea is to create a rumbling effect using white noise and manipulate it through various effects in Ableton Live.

Try this:
1. Create a short clicky kick drum.
2. Generate or download white noise.
3. Bounce the white noise into the drum rack.
4. Trigger the white noise with each kick drum, holding down the gate to loop the sound.
5. Apply EQ and volume shaper effects to enhance the rumbling.

Why it matters:
This technique allows producers to add a distinctive rhythmic texture to their tracks without using traditional percussion instruments, adding an extra layer of complexity and interest to the music.

Common mistakes:
- Not properly adjusting the start position in the white noise sample.
- Overcomplicating with too many effects or not applying them correctly.
