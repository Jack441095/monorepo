Short answer:
Start by creating custom stems for your risers and falls to avoid the need for extensive rework when remixing.

Try this:
1. Create separate stems for each section of your track (e.g., bass, lead, percussion) with specific effects applied.
2. Import these stems into a new project for remixing.
3. Adjust as needed without having to recreate everything from scratch.
4. This approach helps maintain consistency and reduces the risk of needing extensive rework when making changes.

Why it matters:
Custom stems allow you to quickly adapt your track's feel by simply importing them, saving time and reducing frustration during the remix process.

Common mistakes:
- Not creating custom stems for risers and falls can lead to a lack of flexibility in your remix.
- Overcomplicating the remixing process by trying to recreate every element from scratch can be overwhelming and time-consuming.
