# Next Step Mix Diagnosis

Type: Mixing workflow
Tags: mix diagnosis, workflow, reference, balance, troubleshooting, mixing
Status: Approved

Short answer:
When you do not know what to do next in a mix, diagnose balance first: level, pan, low-end, vocal or lead position, then space. Avoid adding plugins until you can name the problem.

Try this:
1. Turn the mix down and ask what disappears first.
2. Compare against a reference at matched loudness, not louder.
3. Fix broad level balance before detailed EQ.
4. Check kick, bass, vocal or lead, snare, and main hook in that order.
5. Mute plugins one by one if the mix feels worse than the raw tracks.
6. Write one sentence naming the next problem, then solve only that problem.

Why it matters:
Professional workflow is partly knowing what not to change. A clear diagnosis prevents random plugin stacking and keeps decisions tied to the song.
