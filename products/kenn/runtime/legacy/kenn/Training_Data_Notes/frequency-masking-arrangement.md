# Frequency Masking and Arrangement

Type: Production workflow
Tags: frequency masking, arrangement masking, arrangement, eq, frequency, octave, register, chord voicing, melodic parts, clarity
Status: Approved
Source: Audio_Too studio practice
Reviewed: 2026-07-01

Short answer:
Solve persistent masking at the arrangement and voicing stage before carving every track with EQ. Change octave, rhythm, register, or note duration so important parts are not demanding the same space simultaneously.

Try this:
1. Identify the intended focal element in each section.
2. Mute competing parts to confirm whether the problem is density rather than tone.
3. Move chord voicings, bass notes, or countermelodies into distinct registers.
4. Shorten sustained notes or create rhythmic gaps around important transients and words.
5. After the arrangement works, use modest EQ and automation for the remaining moment-specific overlap.

Why it matters:
EQ can reduce energy but cannot make two equally dense parts occupy the same musical role clearly. Separation created in the composition remains robust across playback systems.
