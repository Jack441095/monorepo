# Room Acoustics and Monitor Placement

Type: Studio setup
Tags: acoustics, monitors, speakers, room modes, bass traps, listening position
Status: Approved
Source: Audio_Too studio practice
Reviewed: 2026-07-01

Short answer:
Build a symmetrical listening setup, place the monitors and head at the points of an equilateral triangle, and treat early reflections and corners before relying on corrective EQ.

Try this:
1. Face the short wall so the speakers fire down the room's longer dimension when the room allows it.
2. Keep left and right speaker distances to nearby boundaries symmetrical and avoid placing the listening position exactly halfway along the room.
3. Aim tweeters at ear height and match the speaker-to-speaker distance to the distance from each speaker to the listener.
4. Use broadband absorption at first-reflection points and thicker bass trapping in corners.
5. Measure several nearby positions and confirm decisions on headphones and other playback systems; do not EQ a narrow null caused by cancellation.

Why it matters:
Untreated reflections distort stereo imaging and room modes misrepresent low end. Physical placement and absorption address causes that monitor EQ alone cannot fix across the listening area.
