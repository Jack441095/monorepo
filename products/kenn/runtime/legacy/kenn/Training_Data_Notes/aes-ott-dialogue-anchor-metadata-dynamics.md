# AES OTT Loudness: Dialogue Anchor, Metadata, and Dynamic Range


Short answer:
OTT loudness delivery depends on region, content type, and metadata capability. Full-programme integrated measurement is common, while dialogue or another anchor element can be appropriate for specified long-form or wide-dynamic-range conditions.

Try this:
1. Identify the delivery region, short-form or long-form programme type, channel format, and operator specification.
2. Determine whether measurement is full programme or anchor based; do not switch methods merely to obtain a preferred number.
3. Measure and report programme or anchor loudness, true peak, and relevant dynamic-range information.
4. Supply matching loudness and dynamic-range metadata when the distribution chain supports it.
5. Test a path without reliable metadata because some consumer devices or services may ignore it.

Why it matters:
Metadata can describe how content should be reproduced, but compatibility is not universal. A robust delivery works predictably when metadata is honoured and when it is not.

Common mistakes:
- Using dialogue-anchor measurement for content or regions that require full-programme measurement.
- Assuming metadata will compensate for an unsuitable or clipped audio master everywhere.

When this does not apply:
Music-only streaming and cinema delivery use different programme assumptions. Follow their current specifications rather than OTT guidance by analogy.
