# Random Grain Looping and Phase Plan

Type: Mixing technique
Tags: Sample looping, Pitch shifting, Random modulation, Grains, Phase plan presets
Status: Approved
Reviewed: 2026-07-01

Short answer:
This technique involves creating loops of random grains that can be pitched up and down, with phase plan presets adding a unique texture to the mix.

Try this:
1. Loop through your audio file in Ableton Live.
2. Use the "Looping" effect to create looping grains.
3. Apply pitch shifting effects like the "Com Filter" plugin.
4. Insert a "Phase Plan Preset" for added depth and complexity.

Why it matters:
Random grain loops can add an extra layer of texture and interest to your tracks, making them more engaging and dynamic.

Common mistakes:
- Not adjusting the scan width properly to avoid unwanted silence in samples.
- Overusing effects without considering the overall balance of the mix.
