# Recording Levels at 24-Bit

Type: Recording workflow
Tags: recording level, gain staging, 24-bit, clipping, preamp, peaks, headroom
Status: Approved
Source: Audio_Too studio practice
Reviewed: 2026-07-01

Short answer:
Set preamp gain so normal performance peaks sit roughly between -18 and -10 dBFS, with louder unpredictable sources allowed more margin. There is no need to record close to 0 dBFS at 24-bit.

Try this:
1. Ask for the loudest realistic section before setting gain.
2. Adjust the hardware preamp, not a DAW fader after conversion, to establish the captured level.
3. Leave at least 10 dB of peak margin for dynamic singers and instruments when possible.
4. Check every input independently and disable unnecessary digital makeup gain.
5. If the analogue preamp itself is noisy, improve source level, microphone choice, or placement rather than risking digital clipping.

Why it matters:
Digital clipping is difficult to repair, while 24-bit recording has enough usable range for conservative levels. A DAW fader cannot restore a clipped converter input.
