Short answer:
Pitch correction can be achieved by adjusting the pitch of a sound file or recording. This technique is particularly useful when trying to give an extra punch to bass sounds that might not have enough volume due to compression settings.

Try this:
1. Use a pitch correction plugin in Ableton Live.
2. Adjust the pitch slider until you achieve the desired effect without distorting the original audio quality.
3. Monitor the result through headphones or speakers at 4,000 Hz to ensure it doesn’t become too harsh for your ears.
4. Fine-tune the pitch by adjusting the plugin’s parameters if necessary.

Why it matters:
Pitch correction can enhance the clarity and impact of a sound without relying on compression, making it easier to achieve the desired effect in live performances or recordings where volume control is limited.

Common mistakes:
- Applying too much pitch correction that makes the original audio unrecognizable.
- Not listening critically at 4,000 Hz, leading to sounds becoming oppressive and potentially damaging your ears.
