# Mix Review Low Dynamics Repair

Type: Mix Review repair workflow
Tags: mix review, dynamics, crest factor, limiter, glue compression, punch, premaster, revision
Status: Approved

Short answer:
When Mix Review flags low dynamics, back off limiting or bus compression before adding EQ. Restore punch first, then recover level after a matched-loudness A/B.

Try this:
1. Reduce limiter input, clipper drive, or glue-compressor threshold by 1-3 dB.
2. If the whole mix feels smaller when bypassed, check the drum bus, mix bus, and master limiter before changing individual EQ.
3. Listen to kick, snare, vocal consonants, and bass movement; they should regain attack without becoming uncontrolled.
4. Recover output level only after the punch returns, then level-match the before/after.
5. Compare against a reference at similar loudness, focusing on movement and impact rather than peak level.
6. Export a short v2 section and re-run Mix Review to confirm crest factor and punch improved.

Ableton repair chain:
Glue Compressor or Limiter on the drum bus, mix bus, or master; reduce threshold, drive, or input gain; use Utility for matched-loudness A/B.

Leave alone:
- Do not chase extra loudness while dynamics are the main flag.
- Avoid broad master EQ moves until compression and limiting are under control.

Why it matters:
Over-limiting can look loud but sound flat. A cleaner dynamic revision often feels bigger and translates better, even before final mastering.
