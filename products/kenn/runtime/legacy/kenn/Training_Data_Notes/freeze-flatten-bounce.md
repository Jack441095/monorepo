# Freeze, Flatten, and Bounce

Type: Production workflow
Tags: freeze, flatten, bounce, cpu, commit audio
Status: Approved

Short answer:
Freezing temporarily turns a track's devices and clips into audio to reduce CPU load. Flattening or bouncing commits that sound into a normal audio track.

Try this:
1. Use Freeze when your computer is struggling but you may still want to go back and edit devices later.
2. Use Flatten or Bounce when you are ready to commit the sound and treat it like audio.
3. Duplicate the track first if you want a safety copy.
4. After committing to audio, rename and color the new track so the session stays organized.

Why it matters:
Committing tracks can make large sessions smoother and helps you stop endlessly tweaking sound design.
