## Product progression

1. **Qualified private beta:** close all 14 release gates with exact-source
   evidence.
2. **Dependable assistant beta:** accumulate 50+ reviewed real sessions, with
   measured usefulness, safety, reliability, and supported-capability coverage.
3. **Broad capability and musical refinement:** safely promote high-value
   Ableton workflows, integrate specialist evidence such as SLO
   classification, and evaluate across genres and project types.
4. **Polished release candidate:** reach 200+ sessions, calibrated confidence,
   proven recovery/support paths, and three consecutive qualified candidates.

## Core principles

- UX is owned separately; this roadmap covers intelligence, Ableton capability,
  safety, evaluation, reliability, and the contracts supplied to the UX.
- KENN learns through reviewed, privacy-safe real-session evidence—not feature
  count or anecdote.
- Every promoted Live action needs explicit confirmation, fresh target identity,
  authoritative readback, a receipt, and undo or a clear non-reversible label.
- Specialist classification stays advisory and read-only until it is calibrated
  and evaluated in KENN's context.
- Official Ableton documentation may be indexed only from an authorized local
  copy through an explicit opt-in; technical claims cite that retrieval, while
  creative suggestions remain clearly labelled as suggestions.
- Fine-tune the LLM only when reviewed, project-disjoint evidence shows that
  model behaviour—not retrieval, tools, schemas, or prompts—is the remaining
  bottleneck. From-scratch training is out of scope.
