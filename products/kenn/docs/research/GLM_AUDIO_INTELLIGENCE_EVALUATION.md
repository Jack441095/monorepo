# KENN Audio Intelligence Evaluation

The current language layer is a retrieval/template system with LLM rewriting disabled in the public path. Therefore “GLM intelligence” is not treated as a model claim: answers are evaluated for intent, technical correctness, grounding, Ableton practicality, uncertainty, citation support, clarity, completeness, safety, and overclaiming.

The reproducible boundary evaluation is `scripts/eval_ableton_assistant.py` over `chat/evals/ableton_assistant_holdout.json`. On 2026-09-01 it scored 108/108 cases, with precision, recall, F1, clarification, abstention, and refusal accuracy all 1.00. This remains a deterministic boundary/coverage fixture, not a release-quality intelligence claim; it does not establish human usefulness, technical correctness of general advice, or citation quality.

The existing chat evaluator passed its current corpus with LLM disabled and reported 26/26 abstention probes, 6/6 conversation cases, 34/34 diagnostic cases, 30/30 negative-advice guards, and 41/41 public-knowledge cases. Its generated metadata now names `KENN/apps/backend/src/kenn` as the repository-owned read-only evaluation path. These results are still evidence for current behavior, not a human usefulness qualification.

The broader public-boundary receipt is `scripts/eval_chat_coverage.py`: on
2026-09-01 it passed 128/128 fixture checks, covering 84 grounded-answer cases
and 44 expected abstentions. It is labelled `automated_fixture` and keeps
`human_review: pending`; the pass rate must not be presented as a human
language-quality or technical-correctness score.

The next review artifact is [`ABLETON_ASSISTANT_HUMAN_REVIEW_PACKET_2026-09-08.json`](ABLETON_ASSISTANT_HUMAN_REVIEW_PACKET_2026-09-08.json), generated by `scripts/build_human_review_packet.py`. It contains 100 stratified cases, two independent reviewer slots, and ten criteria; all scores remain blank until human review is completed.

Release targets remain: >=90% held-out audio-engineering correctness, >=95% supported-action intent correctness, >=95% citation support, >=99% ambiguity/unsupported abstention, zero unauthorized mutations, and zero absent-audio/absent-Live overclaims. Human reviewers must score the full rubric independently; automated fixture pass rates cannot replace that review.
