   genuinely missing before the fix and present after it.

4. **Built and shipped masking detection** (commit `38f328b`): a new
   capability, `analyze_stem_masking`, that flags when two or more stems
   are competing for the same frequency band at the same time. Deliberately
   separate from the single-mixdown review — it takes multiple named stem

   finished mix. Fully tested (11 new tests) and wired into the live
   server at `POST /api/mix-review/masking`. Explicitly labelled as a
   measured energy-competition proxy, not a perceptual masking model —
