# Blinded Engineer Review Protocol

`KENN_BLIND_ENGINEER_REVIEW_V1` contains 80 randomised synthetic cases. The
review manifest hides detector, expected label, scope, and ground truth; the
answer key is separate. For each case, score correctness, relevance, safety,
and clarity from 1–5; mark unnecessary recommendation, harmful
recommendation, and whether abstention is preferable. Review has not been
completed; the pack is ready.
