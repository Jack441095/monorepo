## Cross-generator robustness

Corpus PDFs are generated by fpdf2 while the app parses with PDFKit — parser
and generator are independent implementations.

## Metrics harness

The corpus suite reports hits / correct / precision / wrong-HIGH per field and
latency, and enforces the V1 quality gates (see TESTING.md). Current results in
NITE_SUBMIT_PDF_EXTRACTION_REPORT.md.
