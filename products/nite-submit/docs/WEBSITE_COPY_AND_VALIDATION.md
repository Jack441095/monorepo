## NITE Submit

**Rename university assignments correctly in seconds.**

Drop in your assignment PDF.
We read the details — your name, student number, module, project.
You check them.
Get the correct filename.

- Works entirely on your Mac — your assignment never leaves your computer.
- Shows what it found and how sure it is. You can always edit anything.
- Use your university's required naming rule — save it once, reuse every time.
- Creates a renamed copy, so your original file is never touched.

No account. No upload. No subscription nonsense.

*Use the naming rule your university gives you. NITE Submit is not affiliated
with or endorsed by any university.*

## Validation plan (small, owner-run; no automated outreach)

1. Would students pay £1–£3 to avoid submission-naming mistakes?
   - 5–10 informal conversations with current students; show a 60-second demo.
2. Do course/university-specific presets materially increase usefulness?
   - Ask participants which naming rule their department mandates; note how
     many differ from our generic templates.
3. Does batch renaming matter?
   - Count how many participants regularly submit multiple files per module.
4. Price test: present £0.99 / £1.99 / £2.99 one-time; record gut reactions.

Decision output: proceed to PRIVATE BETA, adjust positioning, or shelve.
