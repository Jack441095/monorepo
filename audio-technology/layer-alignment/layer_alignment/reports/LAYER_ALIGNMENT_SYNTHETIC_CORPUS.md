# LAYER ALIGNMENT SYNTHETIC CORPUS
### NITE DSP Intelligent Layer Alignment R&D — Candidate Product #2

All audio is synthetic and deterministic. No owner audio. No customer audio.

## Determinism contract

- `case_seed = crc32(case_id) & 0x7FFFFFFF`, `rng = numpy.default_rng(seed)`
- every case reproducible from its string id; manifests in
  `datasets/manifests/corpus_manifest.json`
- fractional/integer delays generated by exact frequency-domain phase ramp

## Signal generators (11)

impulse, sine, multisine (3–8 partials), white noise, band-limited noise,
kick (pitch-sweep body + beater click), snare body, snare noise,
bass transient (harmonic decay stack), sustained bass (sub+saw, vibrato),
synth transient (detuned saw stab).

## Transform library (ground truth recorded per op)

exact_delay (integer+fractional), polarity_invert, gain_db, add_noise(SNR),
min-phase peaking EQ cascade, allpass chain, LR4 crossover split+recombine,
linear-phase FIR EQ (embeds exact latency), mic-like TF (resonances+HPF),
tanh distortion.

## Adversarial set (§30)

pure-tone delays at fractional period positions (110/220 Hz), octave-related
tones, comb-filtered copies (+31/+77), double-transient kicks (gap 2400/7200),
reversed-envelope noise, heavy tanh clipping (drive 10/40).

## Known corpus limitations (documented honestly)

- Synthetic layer stacks approximate but do not equal real multitrack material.
- Parallel-compression model uses feed-forward envelope follower, not a real VCA.
- Room "impulse" for snare composites is filtered noise, not measured IR.
These bias toward *conservative* difficulty estimation for classification
tasks (synthetic unrelated pairs are cleaner than real ones) and are the
primary reason for a CONTINUE-R&D validation stage on licensed material.
