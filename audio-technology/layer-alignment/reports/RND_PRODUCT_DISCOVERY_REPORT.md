# R&D-D — Product #2 Discovery Report

## Method

Weighted score out of 100: User Pain 15%, Market Demand 12%, Differentiation 12%, Technical Feasibility 10%, Brand Fit 8%, Shared Leverage 8%, Defensibility 8%, Time to MVP 7%, CPU Feasibility 5%, Real-Time Feasibility 5%, UX Potential 4%, Commercial Potential 4%, Competition Density 1%, Research Risk 1%. Higher is better; competition density and research risk are reverse-scored. Judgment remains required; this formula is triage, not truth.

## External market observations

- Ableton’s official Live page highlights tagging, whole-library search, neural sound similarity search, and drum replacement workflows. Discovery is already a first-class DAW expectation.
- iZotope RX’s official page positions a broad repair suite with spectral editing, clip/hum/noise repair, stem views, and Repair Assistant; RX 12 Advanced was listed at £1,299 during this research capture. General audio restoration is therefore commercially proven but heavily competed.
- Native Instruments’ official catalogue positions Komplete Kontrol/Maschine around integrated browsing, sound libraries, and fast beatmaking. A new product must own a sharper job than “more sample browsing.”

Sources: https://www.ableton.com/en/live/ ; https://www.izotope.com/en/products/rx.html ; https://www.native-instruments.com/en/catalog/komplete/

## Decision

**Recommended Product #2: Intelligent Layer Alignment**, with a phased wedge: offline analysis of transient, polarity, timing, and spectral overlap, followed by a conservative real-time monitor. It combines measurable pain, shared audio-analysis leverage, and a narrower technical problem than a universal sampler or restoration suite.

**Most technically interesting:** Source reconstruction sampler.

**Most commercially attractive:** Intelligent layer alignment, because it attacks repeated manual editing in a familiar Ableton workflow and can provide value without solving universal timbre-preserving pitch shift.

**Confidence:** 67/100. Evidence is directional: official competitor positioning and internal DSP feasibility signals, not customer interviews or willingness-to-pay data.
